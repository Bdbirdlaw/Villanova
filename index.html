About

Built by Wildcats.

"The fans built the program. Now the fans help fund it."

Three Villanova grads got tired of giving 25% of every ticket resale to a Silicon Valley middleman that gave nothing back to the program. So we built a smaller, simpler thing.

10% of every transaction goes directly to the VASE Fund — Villanova Athletics Strategic Excellence. No asterisks. No fine print.

And every Main Line partner on our deals board commits to the same: the dollar value of every discount they give a fan goes straight to VASE. You save. The program wins. Twice.
