★ About

Built by Wildcats.
For Wildcats.

The night Kris Jenkins released that ball from the top of the key — April 4, 2016, 4.7 seconds on the clock, North Carolina up two — three of us were standing on a couch in a Wayne basement, screaming at a flatscreen, beer everywhere, my mother on the phone yelling something I couldn't understand.

When the shot went in, the room collapsed. We hugged strangers. We cried. We called grandparents who had been Wildcats since the Pinckney era. For a moment, every Villanova fan from the 1985 perfect game to the Scottie Reynolds run for the roses in '09 to the kids in section 109 at the Pavilion was holding the same trophy. That moment is why this exists.

Two years later we did it again. Jalen Brunson, Mikal Bridges, Donte off the bench in San Antonio — the most dominant tournament run in modern memory, and a Most Outstanding Player who'd barely played in 2016. Jay Wright in that suit. We sat in the same basement. We knew we were spoiled.

When Jay walked off the floor in 2022 — quietly, the way he did everything — we knew an era was closing. Kyle Neptune took the reins. We kept buying our tickets. And somewhere along the line, we noticed something. Every time we resold a ticket on StubHub, between fees and "convenience charges," about 25% of the sale price vanished into a Silicon Valley accounting line. None of it went back to Villanova. None of it funded the next banner.

So we built Nova Nation Exchange. Three Nova grads, one basement in Wayne, a lot of late nights and a lot of coffee from Burlap & Bean. We launched in 2024 with one rule: 10% of every transaction, automatically, no exceptions, no asterisks, routed quarterly to the VASE Fund. We added Apple Wallet handoff because the ticket-transfer experience on competitor platforms is criminal. We added the Wildcat-Friendly Deals program because every Main Line restaurant we'd ever loved deserved a megaphone to the fans they fed on game day.

And we built the Wall of Heroes — because if you've ever taken the SEPTA Paoli-Thorndale line to a Pavilion game in February, you know that you are the program. The students dancing in the V-shape at center court. The alums in the rafters chanting "V-U Nova." The Conshy dads in the Brunson jerseys. The kids who saved up to see UConn in person. You built this team. Now you fund it.
