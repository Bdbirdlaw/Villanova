About us.

Founded 2024
Wayne, PA
— Manifesto

The fans built the program. Now the fans help fund it.

We're three Villanova grads who got tired of giving twenty-five percent of every ticket resale to a Silicon Valley middleman that returns nothing to the program.

So we built a smaller, simpler thing in a basement in Wayne, PA. A classifieds board for Wildcat tickets, run by Wildcats, where 10% of every transaction goes straight to the VASE Fund. No asterisks. No fine print.

That's the whole company. Three founders, one mission, and the receipts to prove it.
