The mission

Why the network exists.

The Villanova alumni base is one of the most economically aligned communities in college athletics. The Founders Network exists to formalize that alignment — connecting capital, talent, and operating expertise in a single trusted layer, and channeling its strength back to the program.

— 01

Curated access.

Every member is personally vetted. We approve a small fraction of applications. The signal density is what makes the network worth your time.

— 02

Trusted relationships.

Introductions, deal flow, and partnerships flow through a Villanova-grade trust layer. No cold outreach. No "open networking." Just warmth and provenance.

— 03

Long-term alignment.

Network surplus — events, sponsorships, fees beyond operating cost — flows to the VASE Fund. The economic ecosystem reinvests in the institution that built it.

A note on selectivity

We cap membership growth annually and decline most applications. This is not a marketing posture — it's the entire product. The value of the network is what we say no to, not what we say yes to.

Founder application

Tell us about what you're building.

Applications are reviewed weekly by two members and a network operator. We respond within 10 business days, accept or otherwise.

By submitting you agree to our application terms and review process.

What happens next

Reviewed by two members and a network operator.

Applications are surfaced to two existing members in your industry plus one of the founding operators. Approved founders are onboarded within two weeks of decision.

We approve roughly 18% of founder applications. We decline far more thoughtful applications than we admit.

Investor application

Tell us about your investment activity.

Applications are reviewed by the network operator and two existing investor members. Decisions within 10 business days.

Membership is invoiced annually upon acceptance.

Selectivity by design

One in seven investor applications is accepted.

The investor side of the network is harder to enter than the founder side. Capital is plentiful; trust is not. We hold a high bar specifically to keep the founder side reliable.

Invitation-preferred. If you've been invited by an existing member, mention them above.
